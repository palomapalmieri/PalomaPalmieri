### Paloma Palmieri Alves — Statistician | Public Health Data Science | Health System Resilience

### ⚡ About me

I'm a statistician (ENCE/IBGE) with a master's degree in Population Studies and Social Research (ENCE/IBGE), and a PhD candidate in Health Information and Communication (PPGICS/ICICT/Fiocruz). For about 20 years I have worked with the Brazilian Unified Health System (SUS) information systems, turning large public datasets into indicators, dashboards and evidence for health management.

I'm currently a researcher at the **ResiliSUS Lab**, part of the Center for Strategic Studies Antonio Ivo de Carvalho (CEE/Fiocruz). My work covers health system resilience, healthcare regulation and care networks, patient safety, and the evaluation of public policies such as Brazil's *Mais Médicos* program. I have also worked as a researcher and consultant at IPEA and ECLAC (CEPAL), and I taught Statistics, Probability and Data Analysis for more than 12 years.
